What we’re building.

Is Associate available today?

Associate is a development MVP. Hosted pilot testing is the next stage; this website does not provide product registration or claim that a customer beta is already running.

Can businesses choose their AI provider?

Yes, the product is designed around bring-your-own-model support. Its current server-side adapters include Anthropic and OpenAI. Each live integration needs validation before operational use.

Does AI act without approval?

The initial support workflow centers on supervised trials, independent review, and manager approval. Sensitive actions and broader autonomy are governed by explicitly configured authority.

What does this website collect?

This static page has no contact form, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or advertising cookies. The hosting provider may process normal request metadata to serve and secure the website. Following an external link is subject to that service’s privacy policies.
